# Library of Alexandria: panel text draft

The Library of Alexandria did not end in one fire. It declined over centuries. Caesar's fire in 48 BC, during the Alexandrian war, damaged warehouses by the harbour and probably some scrolls, but the library continued after it.

## What ended it

Funding and patronage dried up under the later Ptolemies and the Romans. Scholars were expelled from Alexandria in 145 BC under Ptolemy VIII. The Serapeum, the daughter library, was destroyed in 391 AD. By the Arab conquest in 641 AD there was little left to burn, and the story that Caliph Omar ordered the scrolls burned appears centuries later and is not accepted by historians.
