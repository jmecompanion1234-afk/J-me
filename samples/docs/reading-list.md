# Reading and viewing list for the team

- Freeth, Bitsakis, Moussas, Seiradakis et al., "Decoding the ancient Greek astronomical calculator known as the Antikythera Mechanism", Nature, 30 November 2006. The Cardiff-led CT imaging of the fragments, with X-Tek Systems' scanner, read the inscriptions and counted the gear teeth.
- Freeth, Jones, Steele and Bitsakis, "Calendars with Olympiad display and eclipse prediction on the Antikythera Mechanism", Nature, 31 July 2008. The paper that identified the games dial.
- Michael Wright, Science Museum London, whose earlier X-ray work and hand-built model established the planetary display argument.
- Jo Marchant, "Decoding the Heavens" (2008), the readable history of the research.
- BBC Four, "The Two Thousand Year Old Computer" (2012), the documentary on the Cardiff work.
- Clickspring, YouTube: a clockmaker rebuilding the mechanism by hand with period tools, over several years.
