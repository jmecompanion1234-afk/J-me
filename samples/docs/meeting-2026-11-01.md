# Exhibit team meeting, 1 November 2026

Present: curator, exhibit designer, education lead, conservator.

## Loan

The museum loan of the Antikythera mechanism fragments and the replica runs until 15 March 2027. The conservator wants the fragments case at 45 percent humidity and the lighting under 50 lux. The eclipse dial (the Saros dial) on the replica will be lit from behind, which the designer tested on 28 October.

## Rooms

Room one: the shipwreck and the discovery, 1901, the sponge divers, the sculptures found with it. Room two: the gearing, with the working model of the pin-and-slot moon gear and the Clickspring videos on a loop (permission requested 20 October, not yet answered). Room three: what was lost, with the Library of Alexandria panel.
